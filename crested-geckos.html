Crested Gecko Quick Facts

Scientific name
Correlophus ciliatus
Origin
New Caledonia
Activity
Nocturnal and arboreal
Adult size
About 8 inches including tail
Lifespan
15-20 years with good care
Temperature
Comfortable room temperature; avoid overheating
Humidity
Misted daily, drying out between mists
Diet
Prepared crested gecko diet; insects optional

Crested Gecko FAQ

Are crested geckos good for beginners?

Yes. Crested geckos are one of the most beginner-friendly reptiles. They do well at normal room temperatures, eat a prepared powdered diet, and are generally calm to handle once settled in.

Do crested geckos need a heat lamp?

Usually not. Most keepers hold them at comfortable room temperature. Avoid letting the enclosure run hot, and ask us about your home's conditions if you are unsure.

What do crested geckos eat?

A complete prepared crested gecko diet mixed with water, offered a few times a week, with live insects as an occasional extra. We are happy to tell you what ours eat before you pick up.
