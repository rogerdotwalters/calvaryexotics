## Meta policy checklist (live animals)
Meta allows ads for live animals from a legitimate business such as a breeder or retail shop, but bans peer-to-peer animal sales and has wrongly flagged business ads before. To stay safe:
- Run the ad from the **Calvary Exotics Facebook Page**, not a personal profile.
- Keep the business signals visible: business name, Tyler, TX location, website link, business email.
- No prices, "for sale" wording, or per-animal listings in the image or post text. Point to the website instead.
- If an ad is rejected, request a review and cite that it's from a licensed breeder/retailer.
- Ads must target ages 18+. To reach families, target parents and adults with pet/reptile interests.
